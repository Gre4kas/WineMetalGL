## Minecraft and shader packs

WineMetalGL 3.6.0 runs Minecraft: Java Edition natively on the arm64 core, faster than Apple's OpenGL, with shader packs. WineMetalGL Settings sets it up in Prism Launcher, the official launcher and Modrinth App, and installs into any Wine or CrossOver bottle on your Mac.

### Games

- **Minecraft: Java Edition**, natively on the arm64 core, outside Wine ([#68](https://github.com/metalsharp/WineMetalGL/pull/68)):
  - A glue gives Minecraft an OpenGL 4.6 core context: SDL3 for 26.x, GLFW for 1.21.x. Sodium and Iris load as usual.
  - **BSL** renders, at 82–84 fps against Apple's OpenGL's 60 (M4, 1708×960, uncapped). Seven of its shaders used names that are Metal or C++ keywords (a function named `vertex`, a variable named `new`), and its terrain rendered black. They are renamed now.
  - Sodium alone: 388 fps, against Apple's OpenGL's 153.
  - Buffer copies keep the CPU's copy of the destination up to date. That removes two full waits for the GPU a frame, from Minecraft filling each new vertex and index buffer from a staging buffer.
- **Shader packs** with compute shaders, image atomics and geometry shaders (Rethinking Voxels, Minecraft 1.21.10 with Iris) went from 2.2 to 11.1 fps ([#68](https://github.com/metalsharp/WineMetalGL/pull/68)):
  - Image atomics no longer copy their textures around every draw. Metal on macOS 27 leaves atomics out of the usage it reports for a texture, so WineMetalGL took two voxel volumes, 12–25 MB each, for textures without atomic storage, thousands of times a second.
  - Programs that declare more than 16 samplers and use fewer link. Unused samplers and images take no slot, and shared shader-pack headers no longer fail with "too many samplers in one stage".
  - Geometry shaders run each listed vertex once, on the GPU, inside the draw's render pass: 586 render passes a frame became 40.

### WineMetalGL Settings

- **Arm64 Fixes**: Game Fixes has a Wine Fixes and an Arm64 Fixes section, with a switch per game ([#68](https://github.com/metalsharp/WineMetalGL/pull/68), [#70](https://github.com/metalsharp/WineMetalGL/pull/70)):
  - **Minecraft** installs the core and the glue and adds the JVM arguments that load them to every Prism Launcher instance, official launcher installation and Modrinth App instance. Turning it off puts each launcher's own arguments back.
  - **OpenMW** installs the glue and the core into OpenMW.app.
- **Installing** has three buttons, always shown ([#69](https://github.com/metalsharp/WineMetalGL/pull/69)):
  - **Install** finds every Wine on your Mac (Wine apps, Homebrew, MacPorts, Whisky, MetalSharp, and any other Spotlight finds) and installs into its default prefix.
  - **CrossOver** finds bottles and copies of CrossOver anywhere, and installs into a bottle even without CrossOver.app.
  - **Custom Install** installs into any Wine and prefix chosen in Finder.
- The app shows as **WineMetalGL**, with a new GL icon ([#69](https://github.com/metalsharp/WineMetalGL/pull/69)).

### Changed

- **Shader math**: shaders compile with relaxed math that keeps arithmetic in the order the shader writes it ([#68](https://github.com/metalsharp/WineMetalGL/pull/68)). SpaceEngine stays correct, without the cost of 3.5.0's safe math. `WINEMETALGL_SAFE_MATH=1` selects safe math, and `=0` now selects relaxed math that may reorder arithmetic.
- `install-crossover.sh` installs into a bottle without CrossOver: it writes the `opengl32` override into the bottle's `user.reg` ([#69](https://github.com/metalsharp/WineMetalGL/pull/69)).

### Notes

- The first launch after updating recompiles shaders and pipelines, so expect normal compile stutter once.
- `WINEMETALGL_PASS_PROFILE=1` logs the GPU time each kind of Metal encoder takes, by its target and programs, for performance reports.
